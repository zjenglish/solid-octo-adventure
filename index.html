Three games in. One win, two losses, and a lot to build on. Updated September 23.

Game 1, Sept 16 at Madison Park
L 0-2

Season opener on the road. Fenway held possession and created chances in the first half. Madison Park scored twice after the break.

Game 2, Sept 18 vs Boston Collegiate (Home)
W 6-0

First win of the year, and a big one. Six goals at Carter Field and a clean sheet for the defense. The home debut for this co-op squad could not have gone better.

L 1-2

A one-goal game against a South division rival. Fenway scored but could not find the equalizer. We see O'Bryant again on Oct 8 at their place.
