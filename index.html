Thank you

What a season.

Boys, congratulations. You made history. First trip to the MIAA State Tournament in Fenway history, first tournament win, 1-0 over McCann Tech at Carter, and a run all the way to the Round of 16, where you went toe to toe with the #2 seed and fell 2-1 in a tough fight. Four schools, one team. Be proud of what you built.

To our fans, teachers and families: thank you for coming out to Carter Field and on the road, for the rides, the snacks, the cheering and the support all fall. You made this a home team. We felt it every game.

Thank you all for a great season. See you next fall. Go Panthers! ⚽

      Player of the Week

      8
      Week 2 · Sept 21 to 27
      Johann Brooks
      Grade 11 · Snowden International

      Johann was our standout in a hard week against two South division rivals, a 1-2 loss to O'Bryant and a 0-3 loss to New Mission. He played every minute of both games, competed for every ball, and set the tone for a young roster drawn from four schools. When the results did not go our way, he kept the group together. That is what we want this program to look like.
