## 🏆 SPORTSMANSHIP & CONDUCT

**MIAA Sportsmanship Expectations** (Updated 8/10/26)
- File: `miaa-sportsmanship-expectations.pdf` in team-inbox/Fenway/Soccer/

**Key Rules for Spectators & Teams:**

**Prohibited Behaviors:**
- Negative comments about participants, coaches, officials (no foul language, names, numbers)
- Storming the field/court
- Leaving designated spectator areas
- Interfering with competition or obstructing other spectators

**Prohibited Items:**
- Confetti, silly string, air horns, whistles, sirens, megaphones
- Lasers, flashing objects
- Fireworks, cannons
- Live animal mascots

**Permitted Items:**
- Spirit items (pom-poms, rally towels)
- Face paint & hair coloring (done before arriving)
- School flags (in designated area only)
- Team run-throughs & breakaway banners (team personnel only)

**Coach & Team Responsibilities:**
- Hold players to high sportsmanship standards
- Lead by example
- Respect all participants (opponents, officials, spectators)
- Follow MIAA guidelines during regular season & playoffs
- Violations may result in removal from contest
